How to reply to reviews, by star rating

5-star reviews

Thank them by name, mention the specific thing they loved, and invite them back. Keep it short and warm.

Hi Sarah, thank you so much! We're thrilled you loved the pumpkin latte. It's a team favorite too. See you again soon! — Anna, owner

4-star reviews

Show gratitude, and if they hinted at what would make it five stars, acknowledge it without sounding defensive.

3-star reviews

Thank them for honest feedback, acknowledge both the good and the bad, and say what you're improving.

1- and 2-star reviews

Apologize sincerely, don't argue in public, show you take it seriously, and move the conversation offline with a direct way to reach you.

Hi Mark, I'm truly sorry about the long wait. That's not the experience we want anyone to have. I'd like to make this right, so please reach out to me directly. — Anna, owner

FAQ

Should I reply to every review?Yes, ideally. Replies show future customers that you care, and a calm reply to a bad review often matters more to readers than the review itself.

How fast should I reply?Within a day or two is best, especially for negative reviews.

Should I offer a discount in a public reply?Usually no. Offer to make it right and move the details to a private message or call.
